Crypto brief · Friday 18 September 2026

BTC above $77k; ETH ETFs still bleed

A day after the Fed’s first hike since 2023, crypto moved with the equity relief tape. Bitcoin cleared $77k and spot BTC ETFs flipped to roughly $159 million of net inflows on Thursday. Spot ETH ETFs, by contrast, posted a third straight outflow session of about $39 million even as ether itself rose. Fear & Greed climbed to Greed at 56. That is a bounce with a flow split inside it — size the risk, do not treat the rebound as a clean risk-on flip.

01

Relief with a split: BTC inflows, ETH outflows

What happened

Cheaper oil and a pullback in the 10-year yield let stocks and bonds firm after Wednesday’s hike. Crypto followed the same signal rather than leading it. Into early Friday Zurich time, Coinbase 24h stats put BTC near $77.6k after a $75.9k–$77.6k range, ETH near $2,488 after a $2,425–$2,492 range, SOL near $105.5 after a $99.5–$106 range, and XRP near $1.32. According to SoSoValue via CoinDesk, U.S. spot BTC ETFs took in about $159 million on Thursday while spot ETH ETFs lost about $39 million — a third consecutive withdrawal day after roughly $224 million Wednesday and $141 million Tuesday. XRP funds lost about $5 million. Fear & Greed printed 56 Greed, up from Neutral 50 on Thursday’s brief.

What it means

Price and flow are not telling the same story across majors. BTC is getting fresh spot ETF demand again after the midweek redemption wave; ETH is still seeing product redemptions even as the token bounces with the tape. That split matters for ETHA and peer ETH ETF holders, for ethereum beta in COIN and related exchanges, and for miners that still trade more like leveraged BTC. A broad token bounce funded by oil-eased risk appetite is not the same as confirmed ETH product demand.

Chart and trend facts

BTC reclaimed and held above $77k on the Coinbase window. ETH and SOL outperformed on percentage terms inside the bounce, but ETH’s ETF tape remains an outflow streak. Structure is still post-Clarity, post-hike digestion — not a fresh weekly trend claim.

What to do

Treat Thursday’s relief as digestion of a priced-in hike against cheaper oil, not as a green light to add size. Prefer confirmed structure you already hold over chasing the open of a Friday bounce.

02

ETH featured: price up, product flows still red

What happened

Ether rose about 1.9% on the Coinbase 24h window to roughly $2,488 while its U.S. spot ETFs posted a third day of net outflows (~$39 million Thursday). Bitcoin’s product side flipped the other way with ~$159 million of inflows the same session. Listed crypto proxies participated in Thursday’s equity relief: COIN closed about $173.97 after Wednesday’s $164.51 Fed-day print, MSTR about $132.25, MARA about $11.64, and RIOT about $21.88. SOL led the majors on percentage terms near $105.5 (+~5.5% from the ~$100 open).

What it means

For ETH itself, a bounce without matching ETF demand is fragile — redemptions can still press the tape when the equity relief fades. Beneficiaries of a cleaner BTC flow recovery are BTC spot products and BTC-proximate names (MSTR, miners) more than ETH beta. Exchange names like COIN can bounce with volume and risk appetite, but they still carry the Clarity setback and higher-for-longer rate path from earlier this week. Do not read Thursday’s equity rebound as proof that ETH product demand has turned.

The open risk is that spot ETH ETF redemptions continue while the token rides equity-and-oil beta — a fade back through the flip with BTC losing $77k would argue the bounce was beta, not ETH-specific demand.

What to do

Size down, not up. If you already hold confirmed ETH or BTC structure, hold it and respect the flip. New risk into a Friday bounce while ETH ETFs are still redeeming is optional — keep size small or wait for flows and price to agree.
