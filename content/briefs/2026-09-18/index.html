Tech bounced; chips led; banks barely moved

Thursday repaired Wednesday’s hawkish hangover: Nasdaq jumped, semis ripped, and the 10-year slipped off the 5% handle. Banks barely budged. Friday’s futures are soft into the weekend — that is a cooldown, not a chase.

01

Thursday rebound: indexes up, yields and oil ease, banks flat

What happened

Cash hours reversed Wednesday’s post-FOMC bruise. The Dow closed 51,779.85 (+0.62%), the S&P 500 7,637.74 (+1.14%), and the Nasdaq Composite 26,418.30 (+1.69%). Tech carried the tape. The 10-year eased to about 4.93% after tagging a 5% handle, oil softened, and the VIX printed multi-day lows. Banks only managed roughly +0.2% after Wednesday’s about −2.3% hit — a limp repair next to the index bounce. Nontrivial October hike odds remain priced. Into Friday morning, US equity futures were soft and cautious ahead of the weekend.

What it means

Digestion after a hawkish 25 bp hike is not the same as a clean risk-on reset. Indexes can rebound while rate-sensitive financials stay stuck — that is exactly what Thursday showed. Soft yields and softer oil helped the multiple, but the still-priced October hike path keeps a lid on how far the bank complex can run. Friday’s cautious futures into a weekend say the market is marking time, not launching a fresh regime.

Chips led the rebound — AMD featured on price action

Semiconductors and AI hardware names powered Thursday’s leadership. The Nasdaq’s +1.69% was a chip-led story more than a broad equal-weight recovery. Banks’ near-flat session made the sector split obvious: hardware beta healed; rate cyclicals did not.

What it means

After Wednesday’s hawkish presser, buyers stepped back into the AI compute complex — Intel, AMD, Micron, Nvidia, and Super Micro — while leaving banks behind. That is a leadership map for who cleared the digestion session, not a blank check to press every Thursday winner into soft Friday futures and a weekend gap risk. Dispersion still matters: megacap GPU beta (+2.5% Nvidia) lagged the sharper Intel/AMD/Micron moves.
