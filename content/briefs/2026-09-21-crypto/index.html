BTC holds $81k after Friday’s ETF surge

Bitcoin spent the weekend and Monday Asia hours above $81k, keeping Friday’s reclaim after U.S. spot BTC ETFs took roughly $433 million on the day. The weekly scoreboard is less heroic: those same products finished the week ending 18 Sep barely positive at about $6.2 million net, while spot ETH ETFs lost about $140 million and ended a four-week inflow streak. Fear & Greed sits at 70 Greed. Into summit week, that is a hold of a bounce — not a fresh chase.

01

Weekend hold: BTC products rescued the week, ETH did not

What happened

Into early Monday Zurich time, Coinbase 24h stats put BTC near $81.6k after an $80.3k–$82.1k range, ETH near $2,664 after a $2,566–$2,707 range, SOL near $112.3 after a $107.7–$113.4 range, and XRP near $1.44. Bloomberg and SoSoValue-linked reports show U.S. spot BTC ETFs took about $593 million across Thursday and Friday — enough to erase midweek redemptions and leave a thin ~$6.2 million weekly net inflow, with Friday alone near $433 million (Fidelity FBTC ~$310.7 million, BlackRock IBIT ~$108.4 million). Spot ETH ETFs, by contrast, finished the week about $140 million in the red despite a Friday rebound near $144 million. Fear & Greed printed 70 Greed, a tick softer than the weekend 71 readings.

What it means

Price held the $81k handle, but product flows tell two stories. BTC spot ETFs needed a late-week surge just to finish flat-to-green; ETH products broke their inflow streak. That split matters for IBIT/FBTC holders versus ETHA and peer ETH funds, for exchange beta in COIN, and for miners that still trade as leveraged BTC. Macro is not quiet either: Bessent–He talks tee up Thursday’s Trump–Xi summit, oil has eased toward the low-$100s, and equity futures opened soft-green — a risk backdrop that can support crypto beta without confirming a new weekly trend.

Chart and trend facts

BTC, ETH, and SOL all print BULLISH with Hold / Trail on the free weekly trend check. Majors are digesting Friday’s squeeze higher, not launching a fresh breakout claim. Structure into summit week is “defend the reclaim,” not “add into strength.”

What to do

Let Friday’s move cool. Prefer structure you already hold over chasing Monday’s $81k hold into a summit-heavy calendar. If you add at all, keep it small and wait for flows and price to agree again.

BTC featured: reclaim held, weekly ETF score barely green

What happened

Bitcoin’s Coinbase window opened near $80.3k and last printed about $81.6k, with the high stretching above $82k. That continues Friday’s roughly 6% cash surge (Yahoo daily close ~$76.4k → ~$80.9k on 18 Sep) after Thursday–Friday ETF buying returned. Listed crypto proxies closed Friday hard green with the same tape: COIN at $194.25, MSTR at $153.92, MARA at $13.24, and RIOT at $23.75. ETH and SOL outperformed BTC on the Monday 24h percentage window, but ETH’s weekly ETF book remains the weak product side.

What it means

A held reclaim above $81k is constructive for BTC-proximate names and for holders already in confirmed structure. It is not the same as a clean weekly flow confirmation — a $6.2 million net week after hundreds of millions of midweek outflows is fragile. ETH beta remains the more conflicted sleeve while ETH ETFs are in weekly redemption. Summit headlines, oil, and Fed-speak can still yank risk appetite before Thursday.
